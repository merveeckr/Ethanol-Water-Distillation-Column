Key Results at a Glance

Operating reflux ratio R = 3.51 (1.5 × Rmin). McCabe-Thiele analysis yielded 9 theoretical stages including the reboiler — equivalent to 12 actual sieve trays at 70% tray efficiency. Feed enters at tray 6 from top. Column diameter 1.60 m at 80% flood (Vmax = 2.05 m/s). Bottoms ethanol loss 0.00015 mol fr. — near-complete recovery. Total Grassroots Capital ₺21.46 million.

Introduction

Context, motivation, and scientific background behind the distillation column design.

🌍

Bioethanol as Renewable Fuel

Fossil fuel depletion and GHG concerns have accelerated global demand for bioethanol. Corn dry-grind fermentation is the dominant large-scale production route due to its high ethanol yield and simple process configuration.

🔬

Non-Ideal VLE Behavior

The ethanol-water system forms an azeotrope at ≈ 89.4 mol% EtOH (95.6 wt%) at atmospheric pressure. This places a hard thermodynamic ceiling on single-pressure distillation and makes VLE data accuracy critical.

📐

McCabe-Thiele Method

The graphical McCabe-Thiele method uses the equilibrium curve together with rectifying, stripping, and q-line operating lines to step off theoretical stages — the most widely applied approach for binary distillation design.

🏭

Project Scope

Design a continuous sieve-tray column for 100,000 L/day of 95.6 wt% fuel-grade ethanol. Covers mass balances, reflux selection, stage analysis, feed tray location, Souders-Brown hydraulics, and preliminary economics.

Materials & Methods

Systematic step-by-step design procedure, governing equations, and key assumptions.

1

Design Basis

Target: 100,000 L/day ethanol · 320 days/year · Feed as saturated liquid (q = 1) · 1 atm operating pressure.

2

Thermophysical Properties

VLE data from Perry's Handbook. Liquid / vapor densities at column top conditions (T ≈ 78.4 °C, P = 1 atm). Ideal gas law for ρV.

3

Overall & Component Mass Balances

F = D + B and F·xF = D·xD + B·xW. All streams converted to molar basis (MWEtOH = 46.07, MWH₂O = 18.02 kg/kmol).

4

Reflux Ratio & Operating Lines

Rmin from Underwood / graphical method. R = 1.5 × Rmin. Rectifying and stripping lines plotted from xD and xW.

5

Stage Count & Column Sizing

McCabe-Thiele graphic stepping. Nactual = (Nth − 1)/η, η = 70%. Diameter via Souders-Brown correlation at 80% flood.

Governing Equations

$$F = D + B$$
$$F \cdot x_F = D \cdot x_D + B \cdot x_W$$
$$y = \frac{R}{R+1}\,x + \frac{x_D}{R+1} \quad \text{(rectifying)}$$
$$q\text{-line}\;(q=1):\quad x = z_F \quad \text{(vertical)}$$
$$N_{\text{actual}} = \frac{N_{\text{th}} - 1}{\eta}$$
$$V_{\max} = C_{SB} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{\rho_L - \rho_V}{\rho_V}}$$
$$D_c = \sqrt{\frac{4\,A_c}{\pi}}$$
Assumptions · Constant molar overflow (CMO) · Saturated liquid feed ($q = 1$) · 70% overall tray efficiency · 80% of flood velocity · Downcomer $= 10\%$ of $A_c$ · Tray spacing $= 0.5\ \text{m}$ · AISI 316L stainless steel

Distillation Column

1.60 m I.D. × 12 m total height · 12 sieve trays · 0.5 m tray spacing · AISI 316L stainless steel · 1 atm · Continuous fractional distillation

❄️

Overhead Condenser

Total condenser · TEMA standard shell-and-tube · Fully liquefies enriched ethanol vapor · Feeds reflux drum · Utility cooling water

🔥

Partial Reboiler

Kettle-type partial reboiler · Low-pressure utility steam · Boil-up: 15,086 kg/h vapor return · Bottoms draw: 26,354 kg/h

⚡

Pre-Heater (E-100)

Heats 29,732 kg/h feed to bubble point ≈ 95 °C · Reduces reboiler load · TEMA standard shell-and-tube heat exchanger

💧

Pump & Piping

Heavy-duty centrifugal pumps · VFD control · Required NPSH for high-volume continuous flow · AISI 316L piping and fittings

🖥️

SCADA Control

Centralized supervisory control · Temperature, pressure, level, & composition loops · Ensures 95.6 wt% purity specification continuously

Conclusion & Recommendations

Summary of design outcomes and suggested improvements for future industrial implementation.

Design Outcomes

The continuous distillation column successfully meets all design targets. Feed rate 29,699 kg/h of 10.8 wt% ethanol broth yields 3,345 kg/h of 95.6 wt% fuel-grade ethanol (distillate) with only 10.5 kg/h ethanol lost to the bottoms — a recovery of 99.7%. The 12-tray sieve column (Dc = 1.60 m, H = 12 m) provides thermally and hydraulically feasible separation. All results are consistent with literature-based ethanol-water distillation benchmarks.

Recommendations for Future Work

→

Heat Integration

Use hot bottoms stream to pre-heat feed — significantly reduces reboiler steam duty and operating cost.

→

Rigorous Simulation

Validate with Aspen Plus using NRTL/UNIQUAC thermodynamics for accurate tray-by-tray composition profiles.

→

Molecular Sieve Dehydration

Add downstream dehydration unit to break the ethanol-water azeotrope and achieve > 99.5 wt% fuel-grade specification.

→

Energy Optimization

Evaluate vapor recompression or pressure-swing distillation to further reduce utility costs at industrial scale.
